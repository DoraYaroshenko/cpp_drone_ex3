**Actions:**
- [MODIFY] `Simulator/src/MockMovement.cpp`

  - *Note: `SimulationRunImpl::run()` already checks for `MissionRunStatus::Error` and correctly enforces a `-1` score, so propagating the error status up through `DroneControlImpl` will seamlessly fulfill the assignment requirement.*

**Verification:**
- **Immediate:** Ensure `DroneControlImpl.cpp` compiles correctly.
